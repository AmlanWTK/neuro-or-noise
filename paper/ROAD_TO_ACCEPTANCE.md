- ~~Do not start Tier 3.~~ **Superseded — see `SECOND_DATASET.md`.** That line
  was too absolute. A second dataset is fine as a *parallel* track with a hard
  1 Sep drop-dead, because the cheapest version of it (V1 alone, no labels, no

  rule that stands: **the submission never waits on it.**
- Do not add seeds beyond 3, more ablations at N=63, or chase significance the
  sample size cannot support.
- Do not upgrade "match" to "beat" — V3 is p≥0.625 and does not survive it.
- Do not add a mechanism claim for the 30–45 Hz effect. Six hypotheses have died;
  a seventh invented at writing time is how papers get retracted.
- Do not soften the "protocol, not people" framing.
- Do not re-cut the paper for space by removing a result. The page budget is
  already tight; if something must go, cut prose, not evidence.
